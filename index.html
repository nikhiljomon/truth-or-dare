⚙️ Rules & Settings

🛡️ The Golden Rule: Safety & Consent First

Every player always has the right to pass or skip. Truth or Dare is about laughter, playful teasing, and bonding — never shame, danger, harassment, or discomfort.

🎮 How to Play

  • Pick Truth or Dare: On your turn, decide whether you want to reveal a secret truth or perform an action dare.
  • Done: Complete the prompt to earn a completion point and enjoy the confetti!
  • New Prompt: If you've already answered this in the past, draw another unplayed prompt.
  • Skip / Forfeit: If you choose not to do the prompt, take a harmless forfeit penalty!
